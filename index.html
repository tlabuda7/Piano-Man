Microphone

If the app is not hearing your notes properly, play the notes it gets wrong, then press this within a few seconds and paste the result to Claude. It shows what the microphone picked up.

When notes are written together, the microphone waits to hear all of them, struck at the same moment. A MIDI keyboard is used automatically in browsers that support it (not Safari on iPhone).
