Jump to a place

Repeat a section

Drag the notes up or down to move through the song. Tap a bar number at the left edge to repeat that bar; a red box marks it. Drag to another bar and tap its number to stretch the box to it. Tap a number inside the box, or the banner above the notes, to stop.

Show

Microphone

When notes are written together, the microphone waits to hear all of them, struck at the same moment. A MIDI keyboard is used automatically in browsers that support it (not Safari on iPhone or iPad).

Songs

Add a song

Load a MIDI file (.mid) or an ABC file (.abc), or paste ABC notation below. Folk tunes are easy to find as ABC at abcnotation.com and thesession.org.
